# Build a Storage Cleaner iOS App

Selection task for the App Builder Intern role | Reference app: <u>Cleanup: Phone Storage Cleaner</u>

## **The goal**

Build a working, shippable iPhone app that helps someone free up storage by finding duplicate and similar photos, screenshots, large videos and duplicate contacts, and deleting them safely after review, on the lines of the reference app above. **Payments, subscriptions and paywalls are out of scope**, so every feature is simply free and unlocked. We are judging how far you get, how well it works and how you make decisions, not whether you copy every screen.

## **Must-have features (the core loop: scan, review, clean)**

<table>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Storage dashboard</b></strong></td>
<td>A home screen showing used and free device storage, and how much space can be freed in<br />each category.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Similar photos</b></strong></td>
<td>Scan the photo library, group duplicates and near-identical shots, mark the best one in each<br />group and let the user select the rest.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Screenshots</b></strong></td>
<td>Show all screenshots in one place and let the user select many at once.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Large videos</b></strong></td>
<td>List videos from largest to smallest with a preview, and let the user select which to remove.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Duplicate contacts</b></strong></td>
<td>Find contacts that look like duplicates and let the user merge or delete them.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Review before delete</b></strong></td>
<td>A final screen showing exactly what will be removed and how much space it frees. Nothing<br />is deleted without approval.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong><b>Permissions</b></strong></td>
<td>Ask for Photos and Contacts access with a clear reason, and handle "denied" and "limited<br />access" without breaking.</td>
</tr>
</table>

## **Bonus (attempt only after the core loop works)**

Compress large videos • Swipe-to-keep-or-delete mode • Blurry photo detection • PIN or Face ID protected private vault • Calendar cleanup • Home Screen storage widget • A "space freed" summary screen • A TestFlight build

## **Out of scope**

Payments, subscriptions, free trials and paywalls • Email cleaning • Clearing other apps' data, caches or junk files (iOS does not allow this, so do not promise it) • Login and cloud sync • iPad, Watch and Mac versions

## **Ground rules**

*   Use **AI tools as much as you like**. That is the point of this role. Be ready to explain what you built and why.
*   Build for iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and test on your **real iPhone** with a real photo library, since the simulator has almost no photos. Keep private photos out of your screen recording.
*   Everything runs **on the device**. No photos or contacts should leave the phone.
*   Give your app **its own name, logo and design**. Do not copy Cleanup's branding, artwork or text.

## **What to submit**

*   The working app: a link to your code repository (Xcode project), plus a TestFlight link if you have one.
*   A screen recording of 2 to 3 minutes walking through the app on your iPhone.
*   A short note (under 150 words): tools used, what works, what is missing and the hardest problem you solved.

## **How we will evaluate**

1. Does the core loop work end to end (scan, review, clean)? 2. Is it safe, with nothing deleted without approval? 3. How fast and accurate the scan is on a large library. 4. How usable and polished it feels. 5. What you chose to build and skip, and how well you used AI tools.
